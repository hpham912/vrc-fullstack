Privacy Policy

Vietnamese Revival Church app (PhucHung AZ) — Last updated November 11, 2026

What the app collects

  • Prayer requests you send: your name (or "Anonymous" if you leave it blank), a title, and the prayer request itself. These are visible to church admins so they can pray for you and follow up.
  • Admin sign-in (admins only): a username and password, used only to sign in to the admin area.
  • Church content you browse: events, verses, sermons and announcements are public church content and need no account.

What the app does NOT collect

  • No account is needed to use the app as a member.
  • No location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, analytics or third-party tracking.

How your information is used and stored

Prayer requests are stored on the church's server and shown only in the admin area. They are not shared with anyone outside the church, sold, or used for advertising.

Admin credentials are stored as salted password hashes, never in plain text.

Your choices

You can send a prayer request anonymously by leaving the name blank.

To ask for a prayer request to be removed, contact the church at htph@vrcfamily.org.
