About the app

PhucHung AZ is the official app of Vietnamese Revival Church (Hội Thánh Tin Lành Báp-tít Phục Hưng), 1565 E Warner Rd, Tempe, AZ 85284. The app shares the church's Sunday worship schedule, events, daily Bible verses, sermon videos and announcements. Browsing the app is free and needs no account.

Accounts

Anyone may create a free member account to use member features. You provide your name and phone number, and choose a password. You are responsible for keeping your password private. Church admins may create admin accounts for church leaders.

Text messages (SMS)

When you register for an account or reset your password, the app sends a 6-digit verification code to your phone by text message. The message reads: "PhucHung AZ: Your verification code is 123456. Valid for 10 minutes." (For a password reset it reads "Your password reset code is" instead.)

  • What you receive: verification codes only, for account registration and password reset. We do not send marketing messages.
  • How you opt in: you enter your phone number on the registration or password-reset screen in the app and tap to request a code. A code is only sent when you ask for one.
  • How often: only when you request a code. There are no recurring or scheduled messages.
  • How to opt out: reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply START to start again, or request a new code in the app.
  • Help: reply HELP to any message for help, or contact the church at htph@vrcfamily.org.
  • Charges: message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile plan. Your carrier is not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Content

Church content in the app — events, verses, videos and announcements — is published by the church for its congregation. You may share it freely. Please do not copy church content to misrepresent the church.

Prayer requests

Prayer requests you send are visible to church admins so they can pray for you and follow up. You may send a request anonymously by leaving the name blank. To ask for a prayer request to be removed, contact the church at htph@vrcfamily.org.
