Child Safety Standards

Vietnamese Revival Church app (PhucHung AZ) — Last updated November 12, 2026

Our commitment

Vietnamese Revival Church has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). The PhucHung AZ app is a church app for sharing Scripture, worship events, sermons and prayer. It must never be used to harm a child, and we prohibit any content or conduct that sexualizes minors or endangers their safety.

What is not allowed

  • Any content that sexualizes a minor, including photos, videos, text or links.
  • Any attempt to groom, solicit or exploit a minor, in the app or through it.
  • Sharing, requesting or linking to child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Any content that puts a child at risk of abuse or exploitation.

How to report a concern

If you see anything in the app that concerns you about a child's safety, or if you believe a child is at risk, please report it right away:

  • In the app: send a prayer request (you may leave your name blank) or use the Contact section to reach the church.
  • By email: htph@vrcfamily.org.

You can also report suspected child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) at report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678, and to your local law enforcement.

What we do when we receive a report

  • We review every report promptly.
  • We remove any violating content and restrict or remove the account responsible.
  • We report suspected child sexual abuse material to NCMEC and cooperate with law enforcement.
  • We do not share a reporter's identity outside the church without their consent, except where required by law.

Contact

For any question about these standards, contact the church at htph@vrcfamily.org.
